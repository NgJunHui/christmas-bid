## Design Context

### Users
Groups of 3–15 friends in one living room at a Christmas party, each on their own phone. One person (the host) often has the game open on a laptop or TV as well. Attention is split between the screen and the person pitching a wrapped gift, so every screen must read in a glance and work with one thumb. Sessions last 30–90 minutes, usually in the evening; nobody reads instructions.

### Brand Personality
**Cosy, cheeky, golden.** It should feel like a lamp-lit living room on Christmas night: deep green, warm gold light, snow drifting past the window. It's playful (a Santa in sunglasses, a "Sold" stamp, confetti) but never cluttered or childish. The copy is short and conversational, like a good host talking.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **References (from the user):** a Secret Santa app with a deep forest-green vignette, a gold high-contrast serif wordmark with a Santa hat on the first letter, gold gradient pill CTAs, gold-outlined pill inputs, rounded green cards with avatar squares, and soft falling snow. A second reference, a red Santa gift app, contributed colourful tile grids for highlights.
- **Theme: dark.** It's played at night, indoors, under warm light.
  - Surfaces: forest green (`bg` oklch 25.5% 0.042 164, `surface` 31%, `surface-2` 36%, `surface-3` 41%).
  - Text: warm cream (`fg` 95.5% 0.022 92), muted sage (`fg-muted` 81%), and faint (`fg-faint` 70%). All pass AA on the surfaces.
  - Gold (`gold` 85.5% 0.135 87 → `gold-deep` 77% 0.152 72): headings, the primary action, credits, and highlights. The gold-pill gradient is reserved for the one primary action per screen.
  - Holly red (`holly`, `ember`): Santa, the last 10 seconds of the countdown, the "Pitching" tag, and destructive actions only.
  - Mint: presence and positive deltas.
- **Colour split:** green 60%, cream and gold 30%, red 10%.
- **Type:** Fredoka (rounded and playful, chosen by the user) for display and headings, semibold to bold, in gold. Geist for UI text and all numerals (tabular, so the countdown never jitters).
- **App frame (Kahoot as the model):**
  - No nav bar. One floating menu button in the corner, plus a tiny "Room 1234" footer.
  - Two-step join: Game PIN first, then nickname.
  - In the lobby, a "Join at … with Game PIN" panel, with players popping in as name chips.
  - The welcome screen is one centred column on the same night-forest green as the game (user request, Nov 2026), with extra falling snow: the Bidmas wordmark (cream, with its Santa hat) centred at the top like a nav bar; the illustrated Santa in a soft gold spotlight; the gold headline "Unwrap the Magic of Christmas!"; then the gold-pill "Join a room" and a quiet underlined "Create a room" link. Desktop looks exactly like mobile.
  - The end screen is just "Thank you!".
- **Signature details:**
  - The "Bidmas" wordmark with a Santa hat on the B; the same hat, on a forest-green tile, is the favicon.
  - The user's illustrated Santa (`HeroSanta`, files in `public/hero`) on the home hero and the thank-you page.
  - A gift stack in a gold spotlight for each pitch.
  - Room code shown as four gold tiles.
  - Festive avatar tokens (gift, tree, snowflake, candy cane, star, bell, cookie, sparkle) in place of photos.
  - A gold "SOLD" stamp and gold/red confetti for the winner.
  - Ambient CSS snowfall, static under reduced motion.
- **Anti-references:** Kahoot's primary-colour chaos, generic SaaS dashboards, clip-art Christmas, glassmorphism, gradient text, neon.

### Design Principles
1. **One thing on screen matters.** Each phase has one headline, one number, and one gold action. Everything else is secondary.
2. **The server tells the story.** Every screen is a rendering of authoritative room state, so refreshing always lands people in the same place.
3. **Theatre at the edges, calm in the middle.** Spend motion on the reveal, the winner, and the countdown's last seconds; keep the snow subtle.
4. **Money is never ambiguous.** Available credits (gold pill in the header), a provisional bid ("Your bid", gold tint), and a confirmed payment (the transfer ledger) each look different.
5. **Thumb first.** The primary action sits in the bottom third on phones, with 48px+ targets and no horizontal scroll.
